Support — Gentleskin

Questions, problems, ideas: gentleskinapp@gmail.com. A person reads it, usually within a day or two.

How do I cancel or manage my membership?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Gentleskin. On Android: Play Store → your profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Gentleskin. Cancel at least 24 hours before a trial or a period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I subscribed, but the app is asking again

Tap Restore on the membership screen. Make sure the phone is signed in to the same account you subscribed with — your Apple Account on iPhone, your Google Account on Android.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the store. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, at play.google.com → Order history → Report a problem.

How do I share my data with my therapist?

Patterns → the share icon at the top → choose a span → Share PDF. You can print it, mail it or save it to Files.

How do I stop sharing usage data?

You → More → "Share usage data". When it is off, nothing about your use of the app is sent to the analytics provider. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I delete my data?

What you log stays on your phone. There is no account and no copy on our servers, so you delete it yourself:

  • One entry: Patterns → Day by day → tap the entry → Delete.
  • A body part: You → Your parts → tap it → Remove.
  • Everything: delete the app. On iPhone that also removes the iCloud copy; on Android, also turn off Backup for Gentleskin in Settings → Google → Backup before deleting, or the backup is kept for a while.

The one thing that is not on your phone is anonymous usage data (see below), which is tied to a random id, not to you or your name. To have it deleted, email gentleskinapp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete it and reply, usually within a few days. It helps if you say roughly when you installed the app, so we can find the right records.

Is Gentleskin a medical app?

No. It is a self-tracking tool. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If picking is hurting you, a therapist who knows body-focused repetitive behaviors can help; the TLC Foundation (bfrb.org) keeps a directory.
