Privacy Policy — Gentleskin

Last updated: July 14, 2026

This policy explains what Gentleskin ("the app") collects and how it is used.

No account, no personal identification

Gentleskin has no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and we do not collect them. Any name you enter in the app is used only to greet you.

Your entries

What you log in the app — episodes, counts, body parts, settings — is stored so the app can show it back to you, and may be synced across your devices so you don't lose it. It is not sold, and it is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Diagnostics and usage

We use third-party providers for crash reporting, anonymous product analytics, and subscription management. Through them we may collect:

  • Crash and diagnostic data — technical reports when the app fails, so we can fix it.
  • Product usage data — anonymous counts of actions (for example, that a screen was opened or a session completed), so we can improve the app.
  • Purchase data — whether a subscription is active, to unlock the app. Payment is handled by Apple; we never receive your card details.

This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. The app contains no advertising and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules occasional reminders on your device.

Deleting your data

Entries can be deleted inside the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To request deletion of anything else associated with your use of the app, contact us at the address below.

Children

Gentleskin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date.
