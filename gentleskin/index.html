Privacy Policy — Gentleskin

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Gentleskin is a tracker for skin picking. What you log is some of the most private information you have, so the short version is this: your picking data never reaches us. It lives on your device and in your own iCloud, and we cannot see it.

What stays on your device

Everything you actually enter into the app:

  • Every episode you log — the body part, the count, the time, how aware you felt
  • Your urges and the ones you got through
  • Your body parts, your daily target, and your name if you gave one

This is stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. It sits in your private iCloud database, under your Apple ID. We have no server, no account system, and no way to read it. If you turn off iCloud, the data simply stays on your device.

There are no accounts

Gentleskin has no sign-up, no login, and no password. We never ask for your email, your phone number, or your name for our own use. The optional name in the app is only used to greet you, on your device.

What we do receive

Three services run inside the app. None of them ever receive what you log.

Crash reports (Sentry)

When the app crashes, a technical report is sent to Sentry so we can fix it. It contains the crash itself — the code that failed, the device model, the iOS version. It is tied to a random identifier for that install, not to you. We have explicitly turned off personal data, screenshots, and view hierarchies in these reports, and no picking data is ever attached.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are handled by RevenueCat, which verifies your purchase with Apple and tells the app whether your subscription is active. Payment itself happens through Apple — we never see your card, and we never receive your name or billing address.

Product analytics (PostHog)

We may enable anonymous product analytics through PostHog to understand which parts of the app get used — for example, that an urge session was completed, or that onboarding was finished. These events are deliberately counts of actions, never their content: we never send how many times you picked, which body part, how aware you felt, or anything you typed. The events are not linked to your identity and are not used to track you across other apps.

We do not track you

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no ad identifier (IDFA)
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing your data with data brokers
  • No location collection

Notifications

If you allow them, Gentleskin sends a few quiet check-ins. These are local notifications, scheduled by the app on your device. There is no push server, and nothing about them leaves your phone.

Deleting your data

Anything you log can be deleted inside the app. To remove everything at once, delete the app — that clears the data on your device. To also remove the synced copy, delete Gentleskin's data from iCloud in Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage. Since we hold no copy on any server of ours, there is nothing left for us to delete.

Children

Gentleskin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date. If a change is significant, we'll say so in the app.
