Privacy Policy — Do Re Box

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what Do Re Box ("the app") collects and how it is used. It covers the iPhone and Android apps and the website at sendamusicbox.vercel.app, where gift links open.

No account, no personal identification

Do Re Box has no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for an email address or phone number, and we do not collect them. The app never asks for, or reads, your device's location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Your tunes

The tunes you make, and the shelf of tunes you keep, are stored on your device. When you send a tune as a gift, the tune, its title, and the "for" and "from" names you typed are written into the gift link itself. They travel only where you send that link; we don't store them on a server.

Usage data

To understand how the app is used and improve it, the app sends usage data to our analytics provider (PostHog): which buttons, keys and screens are used, how far the walkthrough gets, whether a tune is played, kept or sent, the size and settings of a tune (how many notes, its mood, speed and sound), and technical details such as the device type and app version. Anonymous recordings of the screen may be made to see where people get stuck; all text in them is hidden.

Usage data never includes the words of a tune: not its title and not the names you type for a gift (we only see how long they are). It is identified only by a random identifier created by the app, never by a name, email address or phone number, and it is used only to improve Do Re Box. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Like any internet request, this data arrives with your IP address, and our analytics provider derives an approximate location from it (your country and, usually, your city). That is the only location information involved.

You can switch this off at any time in the app, under Look and sound → "Share usage data". When it is off, nothing about your use of the app is sent to the analytics provider.

Crash reports

When something in the app fails, a technical report (the error, the device type and the screen it happened on) may be sent so we can fix it.

None of this data is used to track you across other apps or websites. The app contains no advertising, no purchases, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Children

Do Re Box is made to be played by children, together with the grown-ups who give them the phone. We do not collect personal information from anyone, children included: no names, contact details or photos leave the device except inside a gift link you choose to send. The usage data above is anonymous, and a parent can switch it off under Look and sound → "Share usage data".

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its tunes and settings from your device, and switching off "Share usage data" stops any further usage data from being sent. To ask us to delete the usage data tied to your device's random identifier, write to the address below.

Changes
