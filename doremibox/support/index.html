Questions, problems, ideas: doreboxapp@gmail.com. A person reads it, usually within a day or two.

How do I write a tune?

Tap Keys, then tap the coloured keys: each one puts a note on the strip and moves on. Silence leaves a quiet step, delete takes the last note back. Tap the edge of the keys to slide to the high notes. Help (the ?) runs the walkthrough again.

How do I send one?

Tap Send, type who it's for and who it's from, and send the link anywhere: WhatsApp, Messages, email. They open it, unwrap the present and hear your tune. Nothing to install.

A gift link doesn't open

Make sure the whole link was copied: messaging apps sometimes cut very long ones. Sending it again from the app usually fixes it.

Can I delete my data?

Your tunes live only on your device: deleting the app removes them. For the anonymous usage data, see the privacy policy, or write to us.
