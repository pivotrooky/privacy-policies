Privacy Policy for Release

Last updated: November 27, 2025

Overview

Release ("we", "our", or "the app") is a voice venting application that respects your privacy. This policy explains our minimal data practices.

Information We Collect

Release is designed with privacy at its core. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information
  • We do not require user accounts or login credentials
  • We do not collect or track your location
  • We do not upload your voice recordings to any server
  • We do not use analytics services to track your behavior

Permissions

Microphone: The app requires microphone access to record your voice. These recordings are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to any server. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

Voice Recordings

Your voice recordings are handled with maximum privacy:

  • Recordings are stored temporarily on your device only
  • Recordings are never uploaded to any server
  • Only one recording can exist at a time (the "one-clip rule")
  • We have no access to your recordings whatsoever

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and may collect certain information to serve personalized ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable identifier for advertising)
  • Device information (device type, operating system)
  • General location data (at city level, derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's ad settings. For more information about how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google Uses Information.

Data Storage

All app data (including deferred recordings and app preferences) is stored locally on your device using standard device storage. This data is not backed up to the cloud and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Data Deletion

The app is designed for automatic deletion. Voice recordings are deleted immediately after you choose to "let it go" or automatically after 24 hours. You can also delete all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. All your data remains on your device under your control. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us
